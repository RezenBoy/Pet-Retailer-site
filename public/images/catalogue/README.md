# Catalogue image mappings

These supplier catalogue images are kept separately from the clean product photos used on collection cards. Product records list them in `src/data/products.js` as `catalogueImages`; the product detail page shows them as selectable thumbnails.

| Site product | Source catalogue folder | Match note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steel Dog Bowl (ID 1) | `dog bowl plain 400ml` | Plain steel bowl; 400 ml is one of the site's listed variants. |
| Printed Dog Bowl (ID 2) | `Bowl 1000 Black` | Black paw-and-bone printed bowl. The catalogue set says 1000 ml, which is not listed among the site's variants. Confirm whether this capacity belongs in the product data. |
| Dog H-Stand (ID 8) | `h stand 800ml new` | H-stand match; catalogue graphics specify 800 ml bowls, a detail not recorded in the site's variants. |

Other folders in the source archive include products with no clear counterpart in the current 16 product records, duplicate sets, and nested ZIPs. They were left out rather than assigned based on guesswork. No product names, prices, descriptions, or variants were changed.
